# Enclosure

Mechanical design for the housing and the microfluidic cell holder that positions the
sample cell over the electrodes and under the camera.

- Author source in `enclosure.scad` (OpenSCAD) — parametric.
- Export `.stl` for printing and `.step` for interchange.
- Keep the optical path (camera → cell) and electrode access documented in
  [`../../docs/hardware/`](../../docs/hardware/).
